# Image Generation Prompt Design

## Prompt 1 (Vague): A city at sunset.

### Answer (what the model would generate):
A generic urban skyline with warm orange tones in the background sky. Buildings vary in size without a clear focal point. The image reads as a stock photo — competent but forgettable, with no defined style or atmosphere.

---

## Prompt 2 (Refined): A futuristic city skyline at sunset, with towering glass skyscrapers and flying vehicles, in a watercolor style with soft purples, deep oranges, and pale golds. Wide-angle perspective from street level looking upward.

### Answer (what the model would generate):
A richly atmospheric watercolor scene painted with translucent washes of purple and gold. Glass towers catch the fading light with a soft luminosity, their edges slightly blurred as watercolor bleeds naturally into the sky. Flying vehicles are rendered as impressionistic silhouettes at mid-height. The low viewing angle creates a dramatic sense of scale — the viewer feels small beneath the city rather than observing it from a distance. The overall mood is both epic and painterly, clearly distinct from a photograph.

---

## Prompt 3 (Further Refined with Mood): A lonely futuristic city skyline at dusk, crumbling skyscrapers overgrown with vines, in a dark fantasy watercolor style. Muted teals and deep crimson tones. Foggy ground level. Eye-level perspective, cinematic composition.

### Answer (what the model would generate):
A hauntingly beautiful scene where nature has reclaimed a post-collapse metropolis. Watercolor fog sits heavy at street level, masking the lower halves of vine-covered towers. The muted teal palette gives the ruins a cold, melancholic feel, punctuated by deep crimson in the sky suggesting fire or a dying sun beyond the haze. The cinematic framing places the viewer directly inside the scene rather than above it, emphasizing isolation and decay. The style feels closer to concept art than illustration.

---

## Observation:
Moving from a vague prompt to a refined one produced a dramatically more controlled result. The first prompt left all creative decisions to the model, resulting in a predictable output. Adding art style, color palette, and perspective in Prompt 2 aligned the output with a specific aesthetic vision. Prompt 3 demonstrated that mood and narrative context push the output further — the same structural elements (city, sunset) produced an entirely different emotional register simply by shifting the descriptive framing. Specificity is the primary lever for image prompt quality.
